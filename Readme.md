# MED-INTEL – LabelIntelligence AI

**An Agentic AI-Powered Platform for End-to-End Medical Device Labeling Automation**

## Overview
LabelIntelligence AI is a proposed intelligent platform designed to automate the medical device labeling lifecycle using Agentic AI, Generative AI, RAG, Computer Vision, and workflow automation.

It aims to simplify regulatory compliance, label generation, artwork validation, and change impact analysis across multiple markets and languages.

## Key Features

- **Regulatory Intelligence Agent:** Identifies applicable regulatory requirements, UDI rules, and mandatory symbols.
- **Label Generation Agent:** Generates label content and multilingual label drafts.
- **Compliance & Vision Agent:** Validates mandatory fields, warnings, symbols, UDI, and artwork differences.
- **Change Impact Agent:** Identifies affected products, labels, markets, and languages.
- **Workflow & Approval Agent:** Manages reviews, approvals, version control, and audit trails.

## Technology Approach
- Agentic AI and Generative AI
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Computer Vision and OCR
- Regulatory Rules Engine
- Workflow Automation
- Cloud-based Architecture

## Key Benefits
- Faster labeling processes
- Improved regulatory compliance
- Reduced manual effort and operational costs
- Better traceability and version control
- Enhanced patient safety
